What this is

Your onboarding learns a lot about a person. Your paywall uses almost none of it.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we get to see what actually moves conversion and ARPU across the market rather than inside one app. We pointed that at Fabulous: walked your whole onboarding — the sleep and energy questions, the tracking sheet, the contract you hold to sign — read your App Store listing and your website, and stopped at the paywall.

By then someone has told you the one change they want, when they wake up, how much time they have and which habit to add. The paywall shows them the same four routines it shows everyone, and no price. What follows is five ideas for that one screen and the moment after it, each built into a clickable prototype. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your paywall

The plum background, the gold coin, “Transformation is just moments away!”, the letter from Future Julia, the hand-lettered routine card with its four coloured tiles, the green “Free trial enabled” toggle and the white “Try free for 7 days” pill are all still there. What changed is what they say.
