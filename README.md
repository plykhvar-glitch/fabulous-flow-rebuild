# Growth Audit — Fabulous

**Live:** https://plykhvar-glitch.github.io/fabulous-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic Fabulous already has, all of them on the paywall at the end of the onboarding, plus a clickable prototype of that paywall and an offer on close, rebuilt in Fabulous's own design. Open it on a phone with `?open=proto` to go straight to the prototype. Below the phone you can change the person's onboarding answers and watch the paywall follow.

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Where | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Put the change they asked for in the letter | Paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 02 | Offer your $49.99 annual plan when they close | Offer on close | ARPU +10–15% |
| 03 | Bring your Duke, 96% and 22M back to the paywall | Paywall · proof | CR +10–15% |
| 04 | Show the morning their answers built | Paywall · routine card | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Say the price before they tap | Paywall · footer | CR +5–10% |

## Grounding

Every price, rating and proof point comes from Fabulous's own published material: the App Store listing (4.4 from 89K ratings, Editors' Choice, Premium Quarterly at $39.99 and Fabulous Annual Subscription at $49.99 in the in-app purchase list), Apple's purchase sheet in the live flow (1-week free trial, then $39.99 every 3 months), the in-app "We hear you loud and clear" screen (Duke University's Center for Advanced Hindsight, 96%, 22M) and the paywall's own routine poster.

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Fabulous.
